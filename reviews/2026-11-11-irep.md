## Code findings worth raising with the platform team

- ExportCDT and ImportRBP read ComINT on every cycle; their caching code is unreachable. ComINT itself only serves its appsettings, so changes need a ComINT restart.
- The run tokens in ExportCDT and ImportRBP have no expiry. A hung run blocks that replica type until a restart or the clear route.
- The ExportDCS and ComPBR worker loops have no generic catch. One exception stops the worker. ExportDCS with `CheckIntervalInMin` = 0 spins.
- ImportDES services.json points the next service at a route that does not exist (`ImportDes/ValidatePacket`).
- ComINT logs "default delay of 15 mins" but waits 30 minutes.
- The key `RepreocessIntervalInHours` is misspelled. The method `SimulateApiCall` makes real calls.
- Serilog is set to Error everywhere. About 24% of failure points (575 of 2438) are Warning, Information or throw-only and do not appear at that level. Throws are usually caught and re-logged by the caller, so the real hidden share is lower. Measure it on real logs (PLAN § 5.4).
- Several status lines are logged at Error level ("Process started", "CACHE_CHEKING", "Awaiter Insert status", "Deleted entry … successfully").
